# Work contract

Create this contract before execution. Keep each field short enough to inspect
quickly and update only when a real decision changes it.

## 1. Outcome

What observable result should exist, for whom, and why does it matter now?

## 2. Evidence

What direct observation, artifact, behavior, or test will show that the outcome
exists? Include failure evidence when it matters.

## 3. Scope

What is the smallest coherent slice included in this work? Name the affected
systems, users, files, or decisions when known.

## 4. Non-goals

What nearby work is explicitly excluded? Record tempting improvements that are
likely to reappear during execution.

## 5. Budget

What limits apply to time, cost, risk, complexity, or quality? State the stop
condition or decision deadline.

## 6. Authority

Which actions may proceed, which require approval, and which are prohibited?
Do not infer authority from access to a tool or system.

## 7. Current state

Record the active step, completed evidence, open decisions, blockers, and next
action. Before interruption, add the minimum context needed to resume safely.

## Change record

For a proposed change, record:

1. Reason
2. Effect on outcome or evidence
3. Cost against the budget
4. Accept, defer, or reject
5. Approver when the contract changes
